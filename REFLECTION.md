# Play-test reflection

Write this in your own words after playing a full run. Leave time for this within the two-hour limit.

- How long did you play?
- What worked well or felt fun?
- What was confusing, too easy, or too hard?
- What would you change if you had more time?
- Did you finish a run? What happened?
